How FLAMES works

  1. Cancel shared letters. Every letter both names have in common burns away, once from each name.
  2. Count what's left. Kishore and Priya share an i and an r, so 8 letters remain.
  3. Count around the wheel. Starting at F, count 8 letters and burn the one you land on. Carry on from the next letter until one is left.
  4. Read the survivor. F Friends · L Love · A Affection · M Marriage · E Enemy · S Siblings.

Spaces, digits and symbols are ignored. It's just for fun!
